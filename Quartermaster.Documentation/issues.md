# Known issues / follow-ups

## Motion portal: drafts belong to the user, not to an event

A motion draft is currently created inside one event's portal and can only be released there. Drafting should be a user-level feature: a member writes and keeps drafts on their own profile, independent of any event, and submits a draft to the portal of the next assembly when one is open. This also removes the question whether a draft may still be edited after an event's deadline. Until then the event-bound draft is good enough.
